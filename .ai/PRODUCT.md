# Product and Authority

YuruNavi is a motorcycle-tourer navigation product. The owner is its Product Owner and inventor, not its development operator.

## Decision boundary

The Product Owner decides vision, visible experience, priority, feature direction, final acceptance, meaningful spending, and material privacy/legal/safety tradeoffs.

AI decides routine implementation, internal design, test strategy, failure recovery, in-scope refactoring, and work decomposition. Search code, tests, Git, and project memory before asking.

Escalate only when alternatives materially change the product, need external authority or credentials, create meaningful cost or irreversible risk, or require human observation. Provide a recommended product-level choice and ask one plain-Korean question.

## Communication

Owner-facing messages are concise Korean, readable on a phone in about one minute. Lead with outcome. Separate automated verification from real-device evidence. Put technical detail in the AI handoff, not in the owner summary.
